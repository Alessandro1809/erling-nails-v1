# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Erling Nails serves people in and around Playas del Coco who want careful manicure and pedicure work, including local clients, tourists, and hotel-area visitors who need fast confidence before booking through WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

The website should turn a first visit into trust and then into an appointment. It needs to show quality of work, location, personal care, and direct booking without making the visitor search for the next step.

## Brand Personality

Precise, warm, polished.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic salon templates, pink-purple gradient overload, crowded beauty cards, fake luxury copy, stock-looking glam, excessive glow, and unclear booking paths. The site should not look like a template generated for any nail salon.

## Design Principles

- Let the work carry the premium feeling with strong imagery and calm composition.
- Make booking obvious from every major moment without repeating competing CTA labels.
- Keep Playas del Coco, Five Star Salon & Spa, and WhatsApp visible as trust anchors.
- Use motion to guide attention and tell the service story, not as decoration.
- Preserve bilingual clarity where it helps tourists and locals act quickly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, readable text on video and images, keyboard-friendly links and buttons, reduced-motion alternatives for all animated sequences, and clear labels for phone, WhatsApp, gallery, map, and social links.
